## Steps

### Step 1: Preheat

Preheat oven to 180°C (350°F). Line a baking sheet with parchment or foil.

### Step 2: Sauté the Onion

Finely chop 1 onion. Sauté in a little oil over medium heat until translucent (~5 minutes). Cool slightly. This adds sweetness — raw onion works too but will be crunchier.

### Step 3: Mix

In a large bowl, combine 900g ground beef, 100g breadcrumbs, 2 eggs, sautéed onion, 120ml milk, 1 tbsp Worcestershire sauce, 1 tsp garlic powder, 1 tsp salt, and black pepper. Mix gently by hand just until combined — overworking makes it dense.

### Step 4: Shape & Glaze

Transfer mixture to the baking sheet. Shape into a ~20cm oval loaf with your hands, smoothing the surface. Spread 120ml ketchup evenly over the top and sides.

### Step 5: Bake

Bake on the middle rack for 55-60 minutes until internal temperature reaches 71°C (160°F). Rest 10 minutes before slicing. Serve with mashed potatoes.
