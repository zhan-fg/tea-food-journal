## Steps

### Step 1: Prep the ingredients

Dice the onion, mince the garlic, and dice the red bell pepper (seeds removed). Drain and rinse the kidney beans.

### Step 2: Brown the beef

Heat a deep pot over medium-high heat. Add 15g olive oil, then 400g ground beef in a flat layer. Sear for 2-3 minutes until a brown crust forms on the bottom, then flip, break apart and cook through. Transfer the beef to a bowl and set aside, leaving the rendered fat in the pot.

### Step 3: Cook the aromatics

In the same pot over medium heat, sauté the onion for 5 minutes until translucent. Add the garlic and bell pepper; cook 2 minutes more. Stir in 30g tomato paste and cook for 1 minute to mellow the acidity.

### Step 4: Bloom the spices

Add 8g cumin, 5g smoked paprika, 3g chili powder and 2g oregano. Stir constantly for 30 seconds until the spices are fragrant.

### Step 5: Simmer

Return the beef to the pot. Add the 400g can of crushed tomatoes, 200ml beef broth and the drained kidney beans. Stir, bring to a boil, then reduce the heat to low. Cover and simmer for 30 minutes, stirring 2-3 times to prevent sticking.

### Step 6: Season and reduce

Uncover, taste, and season with salt and black pepper. If the chili is too thin, simmer uncovered over high heat for 5-10 minutes until it reaches your preferred thickness. Ladle into bowls and serve.
