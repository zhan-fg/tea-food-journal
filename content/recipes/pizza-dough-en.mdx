## Steps

### Step 1: Activate Yeast

Combine 325ml warm water (~35°C) with 1 tsp sugar and 7g yeast. Stir gently and let sit 5-10 minutes until foamy.

### Step 2: Knead

In a large bowl, mix 500g bread flour and 10g salt. Pour in the yeast mixture and 2 tbsp olive oil. Stir with chopsticks until shaggy, then knead by hand for 8-10 minutes until smooth and elastic. Dough should be soft but not sticky.

### Step 3: First Rise

Form into a ball, place in an oiled bowl, cover with plastic wrap. Rise at room temperature ~1.5 hours until doubled. Or refrigerate 24 hours for superior flavor.

### Step 4: Divide & Shape

Punch down, divide into 4 equal pieces. Shape each into a ball, cover with a damp towel, rest 20 minutes. Stretch or roll each into a ~25cm round.

### Step 5: Bake

Preheat oven to maximum (250°C+), with a pizza stone if you have one, for 30 minutes. Top and bake 8-12 minutes until edges are golden and bottom is crisp.
