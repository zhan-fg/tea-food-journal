## Steps

### Step 1: Activate the Yeast

Combine 180ml warm water (about 35°C) with 1 tsp maple syrup and 5g dry yeast. Stir gently and let sit for 10 minutes until a foamy layer forms on top. This step is essential for a good rise — water that is too hot kills the yeast, while water that is too cool leads to insufficient fermentation.

### Step 2: Prepare the Gels

Mix 5g psyllium husk powder with 50ml water, stir, and let sit for 5-10 minutes until it forms a gel. Separately, mix 5g chia seeds with 50ml water, stir, and let sit for 15 minutes until jelly-like. These gels replace gluten's binding and moisture-retaining functions.

### Step 3: Mix Dry Ingredients

In a large bowl, combine 200g gluten-free flour, 50g tapioca starch, 30g almond flour, 30g oat flour, 5g baking powder, 3g guar gum, and 1/2 tsp salt. Whisk until evenly distributed.

### Step 4: Form the Dough

Pour the activated yeast mixture, psyllium gel, chia gel, and 30g melted butter into the dry ingredients. Fold with a spatula until no dry flour remains. Cover with plastic wrap and let rise in a warm spot (27–30°C) for 60–90 minutes, until roughly 1.5× in volume.

### Step 5: Second Rise

Gently fold the dough to deflate — do not knead. Transfer to a parchment-lined loaf pan and smooth the top. Cover and let rise for another 30 minutes. Meanwhile, preheat the oven to 180°C (350°F).

### Step 6: Bake

Bake on the middle rack at 180°C for 35–40 minutes, until the top is golden brown. Remove from the pan and cool completely on a wire rack before slicing.
