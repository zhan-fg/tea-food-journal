## Steps

### Step 1: Rinse the rice

![Rinsing sushi rice under cold water](/images/recipes/sushi-rice-01.jpg)

Place 310g Japanese short-grain rice in a sieve and rinse gently under cold water 3-4 times until the water runs nearly clear, then drain. Be gentle — don't rub or you'll crack the grains.

### Step 2: Soak

![Soaking Japanese short-grain rice in the rice cooker](/images/recipes/sushi-rice-02.jpg)

Put the rice in the rice cooker with 1.5 cups (about 360ml) cold water and let it soak for 30 minutes. Soaking lets the grains hydrate fully for a translucent finish.

### Step 3: Cook

![Cooking sushi rice in a rice cooker](/images/recipes/sushi-rice-03.jpg)

Start the rice cooker on its normal cycle. For a heavy-bottomed pot: bring to a boil, then simmer on the lowest heat for 15 minutes, off-heat steam for 10 minutes. Keep the lid closed once done.

### Step 4: Make sushi vinegar

![Mixing rice vinegar, sugar and salt for sushi vinegar](/images/recipes/sushi-rice-04.jpg)

Combine 4 tbsp rice vinegar, 3 tbsp granulated sugar and 1 tsp salt in a small bowl. Microwave for 20-30 seconds or warm gently until the sugar and salt fully dissolve; let cool.

### Step 5: Fold in the vinegar

![Folding sushi vinegar into hot rice with a cutting motion](/images/recipes/sushi-rice-05.jpg)

Transfer the hot rice to a wooden sushi oke (or large bowl) and drizzle the vinegar evenly over it. Use a shamoji with a **cutting motion** (slice in vertically, fold across) — **never press** the grains.

### Step 6: Fan and cool

Fan the rice constantly while folding to bring it down to lukewarm quickly. A glossy vinegar film forms on the surface of each grain.

### Step 7: Hold

Cover the vinegared rice with a damp cloth to prevent drying out. Once at room temperature it's ready for sushi, onigiri, or chirashi.
