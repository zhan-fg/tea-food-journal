## Steps

### Step 1: Prep the Rice

Take 400g day-old rice out of the fridge. Break up any clumps with your hands or chopsticks until the grains are loose and separated. If using freshly cooked rice, spread it on a plate to cool and dry, or fan it for 5 minutes.

### Step 2: Prep the Eggs

Beat 3 eggs in a bowl with a pinch of salt. Pour half the egg mixture over the loose rice and mix with your hands until every grain is coated — this is the secret to golden fried rice.

### Step 3: Heat the Wok

Heat the wok over high heat until smoking. Add 3 tbsp cooking oil and swirl to coat. When the oil shimmers, reduce to medium-high.

### Step 4: Scramble the Eggs

Pour the remaining egg mixture into the wok. Scramble quickly with a spatula until just set (about 10 seconds), then remove and set aside. Don't fully cook — they'll go back in later.

### Step 5: Fry the Rice

Add a little more oil if needed, then add the egg-coated rice. Stir-fry constantly, pressing and separating the grains. Cook for 2-3 minutes until the rice is dry and fragrant.

### Step 6: Combine and Season

Return the scrambled eggs to the wok. Add 1/2 tsp salt, 1/4 tsp white pepper, and 1 tsp light soy sauce. Stir-fry over high heat for 1 minute. Toss in scallions, stir for 10 seconds, and serve immediately.
