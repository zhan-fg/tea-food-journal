## Steps

### Step 1: Prep the Chicken

Dice 300g cooked chicken breast into roughly 1cm cubes. Poached, roasted, or rotisserie chicken all work well.

### Step 2: Chop the Vegetables

Finely dice 2 celery stalks and 1/4 red onion. Small, uniform pieces ensure even distribution and a pleasant texture in every bite.

### Step 3: Make the Dressing

In a large bowl, combine 80g mayonnaise, 40g Greek yogurt, 1 tsp Dijon mustard, 1 tbsp lemon juice, 1/2 tsp salt, and 1/4 tsp black pepper. Whisk until smooth.

### Step 4: Toss Together

Add the chicken, celery, and onion to the dressing. Fold gently until everything is evenly coated. Sprinkle in fresh dill and fold a few more times.

### Step 5: Chill & Serve

Cover and refrigerate for 30 minutes to let the flavors meld. Serve on its own, in a sandwich, or over a bed of greens.
