## Steps

### Step 1: Mix dry ingredients

In a large bowl, combine 150g almond flour, 80g tapioca starch, 12g psyllium husk powder, 6g baking powder and 3g salt. Whisk until evenly blended.

### Step 2: Add wet ingredients

Make a well in the centre of the dry mix. Pour in 30g olive oil, 180g warm water and 10g apple cider vinegar. Stir with a silicone spatula until a uniform dough forms. Let rest for 5 minutes so the psyllium husk fully hydrates.

### Step 3: Roll out the crust

Preheat the oven to 200°C (400°F). Place the dough between two sheets of parchment paper and press or roll into a roughly 5mm-thick round, about 25-28cm across. Leave the edges slightly thicker to form a rim.

### Step 4: Par-bake

Peel off the top parchment and transfer the crust (still on the bottom parchment) to a baking sheet. Bake on the middle rack at 200°C for 5 minutes until the surface is just set.

### Step 5: Top and finish

Remove the crust from the oven, spread with tomato sauce, cheese and your favourite toppings. Return to the oven and bake at 200°C for another 12-15 minutes, until the cheese is melted and golden and the edges are crisp.
