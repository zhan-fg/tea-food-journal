## Steps

### Step 1: Make the Crust

Place 200g digestive biscuits in a sealed bag and crush into fine crumbs with a rolling pin (or pulse in a food processor). Melt 120g unsalted butter and mix into the crumbs. Press the mixture firmly into the bottom of a 20cm springform pan using the bottom of a glass. Chill in the fridge for 30 minutes.

### Step 2: Preheat the Oven

Preheat the oven to 160°C (320°F). Place a baking dish filled with hot water on the bottom rack — this water bath keeps the cheesecake moist and helps prevent cracks.

### Step 3: Make the Filling

In a large bowl, beat 500g room-temperature cream cheese with an electric mixer on medium speed for 2 minutes until completely smooth. Add 300g caster sugar and beat until fully combined. Mix in 2 tbsp flour, 1 tsp vanilla extract, 1/2 cup sour cream, and the zest of 1 lemon, beating just until incorporated after each addition.

### Step 4: Add the Eggs

Add the eggs one at a time, beating on low speed just until combined after each. Do not over-beat — incorporating too much air causes the cheesecake to puff up and crack during baking.

### Step 5: Bake

Pour the filling over the chilled crust and smooth the top. Place on the middle rack and bake at 160°C for about 55 minutes. The centre should still have a slight jiggle when done. Turn off the oven, crack the door open, and let the cheesecake cool inside for 1 hour.

### Step 6: Chill and Serve

Remove from the oven and cool to room temperature. Cover with plastic wrap and refrigerate for at least 4 hours, preferably overnight. Release the springform, slice, and serve.
