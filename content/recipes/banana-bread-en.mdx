## Steps

### Step 1: Prep bananas and oven

![Mash ripe bananas](/images/recipes/banana-bread-01.jpg)

Preheat the oven to 180°C (350°F). Mash 200g of ripe bananas with a fork until fairly smooth — a few small lumps are fine.

### Step 2: Combine the wet ingredients

![Mix wet ingredients](/images/recipes/banana-bread-02.jpg)

In a large bowl, combine 90g egg, 120g caster sugar, 70g milk and 60g neutral oil. Whisk until the sugar mostly dissolves and the mixture is uniform. Stir in the banana mash.

### Step 3: Fold in the dry ingredients

![Fold in flour](/images/recipes/banana-bread-03.jpg)

Sift 180g cake flour together with 8g baking powder, then add to the wet mix. Fold with a spatula using a **cutting motion** just until no dry flour remains. **Do not overmix** or the loaf will turn tough.

### Step 4: Pour and bake

![Pour batter into tin](/images/recipes/banana-bread-04.jpg)

Pour the batter into a loaf tin lined with parchment paper and tap twice to release large air bubbles. Bake on the middle rack at 180°C for 40-45 minutes. Around the 25-minute mark, check the color and tent with foil if it's browning too fast.

### Step 5: Test and cool

![Test with skewer](/images/recipes/banana-bread-05.jpg)

Insert a skewer into the center — it's done when it comes out free of wet batter. Rest in the tin for 5 minutes, then turn out onto a rack to cool completely before slicing. The crumb is soft right out of the oven and firms up nicely as it cools.
