## Steps

### Step 1: Prepare the Glass

Take a large wine glass or balloon glass. Fill halfway with ice cubes. If all liquids are well chilled, use less ice to avoid dilution.

### Step 2: Add Elderflower Syrup

Pour 30ml elderflower syrup into the glass. This is the heart of a Hugo Spritz — elderflower adds a delicate floral aroma that pairs beautifully with the mint.

### Step 3: Pour the Prosecco

Slowly pour 120ml chilled Prosecco down the side of the glass to preserve the bubbles. Prosecco's notes of green apple and white flowers complement the elderflower perfectly.

### Step 4: Top with Soda

Gently add 60ml soda water. Use a bar spoon to lift once from the bottom — just enough to blend without losing fizz.

### Step 5: Garnish and Serve

Slap the mint leaves between your palms to release their oils, then drop into the glass. Add 2 lime slices. Serve immediately.
