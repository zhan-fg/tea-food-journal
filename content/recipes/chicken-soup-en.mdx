## Steps

### Step 1: Make the Broth

Place the whole chicken in a large stockpot and cover with cold water. Add smashed garlic, half a carrot (chopped), half a celery stalk (chopped), half an onion (chopped), black peppercorns, bay leaves, and thyme sprigs. Bring to a boil over high heat, skim off any foam, then reduce to a gentle simmer. Cover and cook for about 1.5 hours, until the chicken is fall-off-the-bone tender.

### Step 2: Shred the Chicken

Remove the chicken and let it cool slightly. Pull the meat off the bones and shred into bite-sized pieces. Discard the skin and bones. Strain the broth through a fine-mesh strainer, discarding the solids, and reserve the clear broth.

### Step 3: Sauté the Vegetables

In the same stockpot, heat 1 tbsp olive oil over medium heat. Add the remaining diced onion and minced garlic, and sauté for 2 minutes until fragrant. Add the remaining diced carrot and celery, and cook for another 3 minutes.

### Step 4: Simmer the Soup

Pour about 8 cups (2 litres) of the strained broth back into the pot. Add bay leaves, thyme, and black pepper to taste. Bring to a boil, then add 200g egg noodles. Cook for 2 minutes less than the package directions.

### Step 5: Finish and Serve

When the noodles are nearly done, return half of the shredded chicken to the pot. Season with salt to taste. Once the noodles are tender, ladle into bowls and serve hot.
