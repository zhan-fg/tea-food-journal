## Steps

### Step 1: Prep

Slice 300g chicken breast thinly. Marinate with 1 tbsp soy sauce, 1 tbsp Shaoxing wine, and 1 tsp cornstarch for 10 minutes. Cut broccoli into florets, slice carrot, mince garlic. Mix sauce: 2 tbsp soy sauce, 1 tbsp oyster sauce, 1 tsp sesame oil, 2 tbsp water.

### Step 2: Cook the Chicken

Heat wok over high until smoking. Add 1 tbsp oil. Stir-fry chicken quickly until just cooked through (~1-2 minutes). Remove and set aside. Don't overcook — it goes back in later.

### Step 3: Cook the Vegetables

Add remaining 1 tbsp oil to the wok. Sauté garlic until fragrant. Add carrot slices, stir-fry 1 minute. Add broccoli, stir-fry 2 minutes until bright green.

### Step 4: Combine

Return chicken to the wok. Pour the sauce down the sides of the wok. Toss everything together over high heat for 30 seconds until the sauce coats everything and thickens slightly. Serve immediately.
