## Steps

### Step 1: Prep the ingredients

6 eggs, 5 cloves of garlic, 2 scallions. Twist the garlic cloves with your fingers and the skin slides right off.

![Prep ingredients](/images/recipes/step01-eggs.jpg)

### Step 2: Peel and mince the garlic

Mince the peeled garlic as fine as you can. Finer garlic = more fragrance released into the oil.

![Twist garlic to peel](/images/recipes/step03-garlic-peel.jpg)

![Mince garlic](/images/recipes/step04-garlic-mince.jpg)

### Step 3: Slice the scallions

Cut scallions into thin rings. These go on at the very end for a fresh pop of green and a hit of sharpness.

![Slice scallions](/images/recipes/step05-scallion.jpg)

### Step 4: Mix the sauce

Combine in a bowl: 50g ketchup + 30g soy sauce + 20g oyster sauce + 35g sugar + 300g water. Stir until the sugar dissolves and everything is one smooth, glossy liquid. This is the soul of the dish — a tangy, savory-sweet tomato glaze.

![Sauce ingredients measured](/images/recipes/step06-sauce.jpg)

![Stir into glossy sauce](/images/recipes/step07-mix-sauce.jpg)

### Step 5: Fry the eggs sunny-side up

Heat oil in your wok over medium-low heat. Crack the eggs straight in — one by one, keeping them whole. Let the whites set and the bottoms turn golden, but don't flip. You want runny yolks.

![Fry eggs in hot oil](/images/recipes/step02-fry.jpg)

### Step 6: Simmer in the sauce

Once the egg whites are set, pour the sauce over them. Cover and simmer on medium-low for 2–3 minutes. The eggs soak up the tangy glaze while the sauce reduces to a thick, glossy coat.

![Pour sauce over eggs to simmer](/images/recipes/step08-simmer.jpg)

### Step 7: Garnish and serve

When the sauce is thick and luscious, kill the heat and scatter the scallions on top. Slide the eggs — sauce and all — over a bowl of hot rice. Eat immediately while the yolks are still molten.

![Scatter scallions to finish](/images/recipes/step09-garnish.jpg)

![Serve over rice](/images/recipes/step10-done.jpg)
