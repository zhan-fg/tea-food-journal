    61|## Steps
    62|
    63|### Step 1: Prep the ingredients
    64|
    65|6 eggs, 5 cloves of garlic, 2 scallions. Twist the garlic cloves with your fingers and the skin slides right off.
    66|
    67|![Prep ingredients](https://aka.doubaocdn.com/s/8sOh1wiuTM)
    68|
    69|### Step 2: Peel and mince the garlic
    70|
    71|Mince the peeled garlic as fine as you can. Finer garlic = more fragrance released into the oil.
    72|
    73|![Twist garlic to peel](https://aka.doubaocdn.com/s/6Xrf1wiuU0)
    74|
    75|![Mince garlic](https://aka.doubaocdn.com/s/dLS21wiuUJ)
    76|
    77|### Step 3: Slice the scallions
    78|
    79|Cut scallions into thin rings. These go on at the very end for a fresh pop of green and a hit of sharpness.
    80|
    81|![Slice scallions](https://aka.doubaocdn.com/s/CqoV1wiuUe)
    82|
    83|### Step 4: Mix the sauce
    84|
    85|Combine in a bowl: 50g ketchup + 30g soy sauce + 20g oyster sauce + 35g sugar + 300g water. Stir until the sugar dissolves and everything is one smooth, glossy liquid. This is the soul of the dish — a tangy, savory-sweet tomato glaze.
    86|
    87|![Sauce ingredients measured](https://aka.doubaocdn.com/s/52U11wiuUz)
    88|
    89|![Stir into glossy sauce](https://aka.doubaocdn.com/s/l5ZA1wiuVK)
    90|
    91|### Step 5: Fry the eggs sunny-side up
    92|
    93|Heat oil in your wok over medium-low heat. Crack the eggs straight in — one by one, keeping them whole. Let the whites set and the bottoms turn golden, but don't flip. You want runny yolks.
    94|
    95|![Fry eggs in hot oil](https://aka.doubaocdn.com/s/qFSv1wiuTe)
    96|
    97|### Step 6: Simmer in the sauce
    98|
    99|Once the egg whites are set, pour the sauce over them. Cover and simmer on medium-low for 2–3 minutes. The eggs soak up the tangy glaze while the sauce reduces to a thick, glossy coat.
   100|
   101|![Pour sauce over eggs to simmer](https://aka.doubaocdn.com/s/ZIzs1wiuVd)
   102|
   103|### Step 7: Garnish and serve
   104|
   105|When the sauce is thick and luscious, kill the heat and scatter the scallions on top. Slide the eggs — sauce and all — over a bowl of hot rice. Eat immediately while the yolks are still molten.
   106|
   107|![Scatter scallions to finish](https://aka.doubaocdn.com/s/QsK21wiuVv)
   108|
   109|![Serve over rice](https://aka.doubaocdn.com/s/XHtu1wiuWD)
