## Steps

### Step 1: Make the potato mash

Steam 2 large potatoes until fork-tender, then mash until smooth. Mix in salt, lots of black pepper, and a big handful of shredded cheese.

![Make potato mash](/images/recipes/meat-fries-01.jpg)

### Step 2: Add corn starch

Knead in 130–180g of corn starch until the mash forms a workable dough.

![Add corn starch](/images/recipes/meat-fries-02.jpg)

### Step 3: Shape and freeze

Transfer the dough into a freezer bag, flatten into a neat rectangle, and freeze until firm.

![Shape in bag](/images/recipes/meat-fries-03.jpg)

### Step 4: Unwrap and slice

Peel off the bag and slice the frozen slab into thick fries.

![Unwrap](/images/recipes/meat-fries-04.jpg)

### Step 5: Heat the oil

Pour oil into a wok and heat to 170°C (340°F).

![Heat oil](/images/recipes/meat-fries-05.jpg)

### Step 6: Fry the fries

Fry the potato sticks for 10–15 seconds until golden and crisp. Work in batches.

![Frying](/images/recipes/meat-fries-06.jpg)

![Fry done](/images/recipes/meat-fries-07.jpg)

### Step 7: Fries done

Golden, shatteringly crisp on the outside, fluffy inside.

![Fries plated](/images/recipes/meat-fries-08.jpg)

### Step 8: Start the meat sauce

Sauté minced garlic until fragrant, then add ground pork and break it apart as it browns.

![Sauté garlic and pork](/images/recipes/meat-fries-09.jpg)

![Pork browned](/images/recipes/meat-fries-10.jpg)

### Step 9: Add tomatoes and seasonings

Toss in diced tomatoes, ketchup, soy sauce, oyster sauce, a pinch of MSG, salt, and sugar.

![Add tomatoes](/images/recipes/meat-fries-11.jpg)

### Step 10: Simmer the ragu

Stir and cook until the tomatoes break down and the sauce turns thick and glossy.

![Simmering](/images/recipes/meat-fries-12.jpg)

![Ragu done](/images/recipes/meat-fries-13.jpg)

### Step 11: Assemble

Pile the fries onto a plate, ladle on the meat ragu with reckless generosity, then drizzle with molten cheese sauce.

![Assemble](/images/recipes/meat-fries-14.jpg)

### Step 12: Serve

Golden fries buried under a mountain of meat sauce and stretchy cheese. Dig in immediately.

![Final dish](/images/recipes/meat-fries-15.jpg)
