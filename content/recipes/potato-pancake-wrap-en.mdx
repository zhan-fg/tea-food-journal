## Steps

### Step 1: Fry the potato base + crack the egg

Brush a pan with oil, spread potato shreds evenly, and fry until set. Crack the egg on top and spread it with a spatula to cover the entire potato base.

![Fry potato base with egg](/images/recipes/potato-pancake-01.jpg)

### Step 2: Brush on the sauce

Once the egg is cooked, squeeze on your seasoning sauce and brush it evenly across the surface.

![Brush on sauce](/images/recipes/potato-pancake-02.jpg)

### Step 3: Snip scallions on top

Chop scallions directly over the pancake for that fresh, sharp kick.

![Snip scallions](/images/recipes/potato-pancake-03.jpg)

### Step 4: Add sausage and fillings

Lay on starch sausage as the core filling — it brings the chew and the satisfaction.

![Add sausage](/images/recipes/potato-pancake-04.jpg)

### Step 5: Finish and serve

Add cooked spicy noodles and fresh lettuce, roll it all up, and slice into segments. A loaded potato jianbing, done.

![Final dish](/images/recipes/potato-pancake-05.jpg)
