## Steps

### Step 1: Cook the Pasta

Bring a large pot of generously salted water to a boil. Cook 300g macaroni 1 minute less than package directions (al dente). Drain and toss with a little oil to prevent sticking.

### Step 2: Make the Roux

In a separate pot, melt 40g butter over low heat. Add 40g flour and whisk constantly for 1-2 minutes until pale golden and nutty. Slowly pour in 600ml whole milk, whisking continuously to prevent lumps. Keep over low heat until noticeably thickened (5-8 minutes — it should coat the back of a spoon).

### Step 3: Add the Cheese

Remove from heat. Stir in 250g grated cheddar (reserve a handful for topping). Mix until completely melted and silky. Season with 1 tsp salt and black pepper.

### Step 4: Combine and Assemble

Fold the cooked macaroni into the cheese sauce until evenly coated. Transfer to a baking dish and spread evenly. Top with reserved cheese and 30g breadcrumbs.

### Step 5: Bake

Bake at 200°C (400°F) on the middle rack for 20-25 minutes until golden and bubbling. Rest for 5 minutes before serving.
