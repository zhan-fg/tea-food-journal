## Steps

### Step 1: Make scallion-ginger water

Before touching the shrimp, steep chopped scallion and ginger in warm water. This aromatic infusion will de-fishify the shrimp later.

![Make scallion-ginger water](/images/recipes/wasabi-shrimp-01.png)

### Step 2: Peel and devein

Remove the heads and shells. Keep the tail if you want a handle — otherwise, go fully naked.

![Peel shrimp](/images/recipes/wasabi-shrimp-01.jpg)

### Step 3: Butterfly the shrimp

Slice along the back and remove the vein. Butterflying helps them curl into tight balls when fried.

![Butterfly shrimp](/images/recipes/wasabi-shrimp-02.jpg)

### Step 4: Butterflied shrimp ready

Clean, split, and ready for the marinade.

![Butterflied](/images/recipes/wasabi-shrimp-03.jpg)

### Step 5: Pat dry

Rinse, then pat every shrimp bone-dry with paper towels. Moisture is the enemy of a good batter.

![Pat dry](/images/recipes/wasabi-shrimp-04.jpg)

### Step 6: Marinate

Soak the shrimp in the scallion-ginger water with seasonings and an egg yolk. Let them drink it in.

![Marinate](/images/recipes/wasabi-shrimp-05.jpg)

### Step 7: Batter up

Mix a light, crispy batter. Coat each shrimp evenly.

![Mix batter](/images/recipes/wasabi-shrimp-06.jpg)

![Coat shrimp](/images/recipes/wasabi-shrimp-07.jpg)

### Step 8: Fry

Drop into 170°C oil. Fry until golden — about a minute. They should be crisp outside, tender inside.

![Fry shrimp](/images/recipes/wasabi-shrimp-08.jpg)

### Step 9: Make the honey wasabi sauce

Mayonnaise is the base. Add honey mustard and stir until smooth. Taste and adjust — you want sweet upfront, wasabi heat trailing.

![Mix sauce 1](/images/recipes/wasabi-shrimp-09.jpg)

![Mix sauce 2](/images/recipes/wasabi-shrimp-10.jpg)

### Step 10: Toss and plate

Dump the hot shrimp balls into the sauce and toss until every one is glossy and coated. Plate up and shower with parsley flakes.

![Toss in sauce](/images/recipes/wasabi-shrimp-11.jpg)

![Final dish](/images/recipes/wasabi-shrimp-12.jpg)
