## Steps

### Step 1: Rinse rice, season with soy sauce

Rinse the rice and place it in the rice cooker. Add a splash of soy sauce and stir so every grain picks up color and flavor.

![Rice with soy sauce](/images/recipes/lazy-rice-01.jpg)

### Step 2: Layer on all the toppings

Arrange diced carrot, diced potato, sliced shiitake, sliced Chinese sausage, corn, and peas right on top of the rice. Drizzle with sesame oil.

![Layer toppings](/images/recipes/lazy-rice-02.jpg)

### Step 3: Add water and start cooking

Pour in just enough water to barely cover everything. Give it a gentle stir, close the lid, and hit the regular cook setting.

![Add water](/images/recipes/lazy-rice-03.jpg)

### Step 4: Stir it all together

When the cooker clicks off, open the lid and stir everything vigorously. Every grain should be glistening, every bite loaded.

![Stir together](/images/recipes/lazy-rice-04.jpg)

### Step 5: Serve with soup

Ladle the rice into bowls. Pair with a simple seaweed egg drop soup — one spoonful of rice, one sip of soup, and you've got a meal.

![Serve with soup](/images/recipes/lazy-rice-05.jpg)
