## Steps

### Step 1: Preheat and prep dry ingredients

Preheat the oven to 190°C (375°F). Whisk together 2¼ cups all-purpose flour, 1 tsp baking soda and ½ tsp salt; set aside.

### Step 2: Cream butter and sugars

In a large bowl, beat 1 cup softened unsalted butter with ¾ cup granulated sugar and ¾ cup brown sugar on medium-high until pale, fluffy and lightened (about 2-3 minutes).

### Step 3: Add egg and vanilla

Add 1 egg and 1 tsp vanilla extract; beat on medium until fully incorporated and smooth.

### Step 4: Mix in the flour

Add the dry ingredients in two additions, mixing on low or folding with a spatula just until no dry flour remains. **Don't overmix.**

### Step 5: Fold in the chips

Stir in 2 cups semisweet chocolate chips (and 1 cup chopped walnuts, if using) with a spatula until evenly distributed.

### Step 6: Scoop and bake

Drop or scoop the dough into balls, spacing about 5cm (2 inches) apart on ungreased cookie sheets. Bake one sheet at a time on the middle rack for 8-10 minutes, until the edges are light golden and the centers still look slightly soft.

### Step 7: Cool

Let the cookies rest on the sheet for 2 minutes to set, then transfer to a cooling rack. The seemingly underdone centers will turn chewy and crisp as they cool.
