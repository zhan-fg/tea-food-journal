## Steps

### Step 1: Warm your teaware

![Warm your teaware](/images/recipes/chenpi-puer-01.jpg)

Set up a gaiwan, fairness pitcher, and tasting cup. Rinse everything with hot water to warm it up.

### Step 2: Break the chenpi

![Break the chenpi](/images/recipes/chenpi-puer-02.jpg)

Take about 3g of aged tangerine peel and break it into small pieces. Older chenpi has a deeper, rounder fragrance.

### Step 3: Add the leaves

![Add the leaves](/images/recipes/chenpi-puer-03.jpg)

Place 5g of ripe pu-erh and the broken chenpi pieces into the gaiwan.

### Step 4: Rinse

![Rinse](/images/recipes/chenpi-puer-04.jpg)

Pour in water at 95°C, then immediately pour it out. This quick rinse wakes up the tea and washes away any storage notes.

### Step 5: Brew

![Brew](/images/recipes/chenpi-puer-05.jpg)

Pour in fresh 95°C water and steep for 15–20 seconds. Decant into the fairness pitcher.

### Step 6: Taste

Pour into your cup and breathe it in — the chenpi's citrus hits your nose first, then the pu-erh's deep, earthy body fills your mouth. This tea easily goes 8–10 steeps, each one a little different.
