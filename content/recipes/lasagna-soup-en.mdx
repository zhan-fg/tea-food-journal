## Steps

### Step 1: Brown the Beef

Heat 1 tbsp olive oil in a large pot over medium-high heat. Add 450g ground beef and chopped onion. Cook until beef is browned and onion is translucent, about 5-6 minutes. Add minced garlic and cook 30 seconds more. Drain excess fat.

### Step 2: Build the Broth

Pour in 680g marinara sauce, 1440ml beef broth, 400g canned diced tomatoes (with juice), 2 tbsp tomato paste, and 2 tsp Italian seasoning. Stir well, bring to a boil, then reduce to medium-low. Cover and simmer 10 minutes.

### Step 3: Cook the Noodles

Break lasagna noodles into irregular 3-4cm pieces and add to the pot. Cook for 10-12 minutes until tender, stirring occasionally to prevent sticking.

### Step 4: Add the Cheese

Remove from heat. Stir in 120ml heavy cream. Sprinkle in grated Parmesan and torn mozzarella. Cover and let sit for 1 minute until cheese melts.

### Step 5: Serve

Ladle into bowls and top with extra Parmesan. Serve hot.
