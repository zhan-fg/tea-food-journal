## Steps

### Step 1: Prep

Rinse 10 red dates and break each one open. Slice about 10g of fresh ginger into 3–5 thin coins — no need to peel.

### Step 2: Cook the dates

Add the broken dates to your kettle with 1000ml of water. Bring to a boil, then boil for 10 minutes.

### Step 3: Low-temp steep

Switch to keep-warm mode and steep for 4–5 hours. This is the same foundation as classic red date tea.

### Step 4: Add the ginger

Now add the ginger slices. Bring back to a boil and let it go for 10 minutes.

> Why add ginger last? Ginger turns harsh and acrid when boiled for hours. A quick 10-minute steep at the end gives you all the warmth and fragrance without the bite.

### Step 5: Serve

The tea comes out a deeper amber than plain red date tea, with a gentle ginger fragrance. First sip is sweet, then a subtle warmth spreads — cosy, not spicy.
