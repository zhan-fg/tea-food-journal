## Steps

### Step 1: Layer pineapple and ribs in the pressure cooker

Line the bottom of the pressure cooker with chopped pineapple, then lay the ribs on top. The fruit's fragrance will infuse the meat from below.

![Layer pineapple and ribs](/images/recipes/pineapple-ribs-01.jpg)

### Step 2: Add scallion and sauces

Toss in scallion sections, then drizzle over oyster sauce, soy sauce, and dark soy sauce. No water needed — the pineapple will release its own.

![Add scallion and sauces](/images/recipes/pineapple-ribs-02.jpg)

### Step 3: Pressure cook for 12 minutes

Seal the lid, bring to high pressure, then lower the heat and cook for 12 minutes. The ribs will be fork-tender.

![Pressure cook](/images/recipes/pineapple-ribs-03.jpg)

### Step 4: Release, reduce, serve

Release the pressure, open the lid, and turn the heat to high. Reduce the sauce until it's thick and glossy, coating every rib. The kitchen will smell incredible.

![Reduce and serve](/images/recipes/pineapple-ribs-04.jpg)
