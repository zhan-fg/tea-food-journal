## Steps

### Step 1: Melt Chocolate & Butter

Place 85g unsweetened chocolate, 145g semi-sweet chocolate, and 290g butter in a heatproof bowl. Melt over a double boiler or microwave in short 20-second bursts, stirring until smooth. Set aside to cool to room temperature.

### Step 2: Whip the Eggs

In a large bowl, combine 175g eggs, 260g caster sugar, 2g salt, and 7ml vanilla extract. Beat with a whisk on medium speed for 3-5 minutes until pale, thick, and fluffy.

### Step 3: Combine

Pour the cooled chocolate mixture into the egg mixture. Mix on low until just combined. Sift in 115g flour and fold gently with a spatula until no dry streaks remain. Add 115g walnut pieces and fold a couple more times.

### Step 4: Bake

Pour the batter into a 20cm square pan lined with parchment paper. Smooth the top. Bake at 190°C (375°F) on the middle rack for 45-50 minutes. A toothpick inserted in the center should come out with moist crumbs attached.

### Step 5: Cool & Slice

Cool completely in the pan (at least 2 hours). For the best texture, refrigerate overnight before slicing.
