## Steps

### Step 1: Rinse and soak

Rinse 1.6kg pork leg bones and 600g pork hock under running water. Soak in a large bowl of cold water for 15 minutes to draw out the blood, then drain. This step determines how clean and white the broth turns out.

### Step 2: Load the pot

Place the bones, hock, 1 whole head of garlic (halved crosswise) and 1 knob of ginger (smashed) in the pressure cooker insert. Add 1.9L cold water — just enough to cover the bones.

### Step 3: Pressure cook

Lock the lid and cook on High pressure for 60 minutes. Carefully quick-release the steam valve afterwards (mind the hot vapor).

### Step 4: Skim and roll

Open the lid and skim off the scum with a fine-mesh skimmer. Keep the broth at a rolling boil on Sauté-high for about 20 minutes, skimming now and then — sustained boiling emulsifies the fat and turns the soup milky white.

### Step 5: Strain and press

Strain the bones and broth into another pot. Press the bones firmly with a wooden spatula to push the liquid trapped in the crevices back into the broth, but don't scrape off the marrow fat.

### Step 6: Ready to use

The milky broth is now ready as a ramen base — season to taste with salt or soy sauce. Pair with alkaline noodles, chashu, and a soft-boiled egg for a full tonkotsu bowl.
