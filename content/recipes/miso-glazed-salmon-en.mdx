## Steps

### Step 1: Make the miso glaze

In a small bowl, combine 50g white miso, 30g mirin, 20g sake, 10g sugar and 8g soy sauce. Stir until smooth with no lumps.

### Step 2: Marinate the salmon

Pat the salmon fillets dry with kitchen paper. Brush the miso glaze evenly over all sides (you can skip the skin side). Cover loosely with cling film and let rest at room temperature for 10 minutes. Don't leave the salmon in miso for too long or it will turn overly salty.

### Step 3: Prep for the pan

Gently wipe off excess glaze with kitchen paper — a thin coating is all you want; a thick layer will burn. Make sure the fillets have come up to room temperature.

### Step 4: Sear

Heat 8g sesame oil in a nonstick frying pan over medium-low heat. Place the salmon skin-side down and cook for about 4 minutes until the skin is golden and crisp. Don't move the fillets around.

### Step 5: Flip and finish

Carefully flip the fillets and cook for another 3-4 minutes until the surface is caramelised and the centre is just barely opaque (still slightly pink in the middle is perfect). Plate up and garnish with white sesame seeds and sliced scallion.
